Karnataka Tourism - Government Services

Government Services for Tourists

  • Online Travel Permits: Apply for forest, heritage, or restricted area permits.
  • Local Guide Booking: Certified multilingual guides for tourist sites.
  • Hotel Recommendations: Verified government-approved accommodations.
  • Tourist Grievance Portal: File complaints and track responses.
  • Lost & Found Service: Help for lost belongings and documents.
  • Emergency Helpline: 24x7 support for tourists in distress.
