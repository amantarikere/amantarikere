Discover Karnataka

Karnataka is a land of diverse culture, heritage, and natural beauty. Explore historic palaces, lush hills, beaches, and vibrant festivals. This portal also enables you to access government services easily from your home.

Top Tourist Attractions

Mysore Palace

The magnificent Mysore Palace is a stunning example of Indo-Saracenic architecture and a key attraction in Karnataka.

Hampi

Explore the ancient ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire, a UNESCO World Heritage Site filled with magnificent temples and monuments.

Coorg

Known as the Scotland of India, Coorg offers lush coffee plantations, waterfalls, and breathtaking hills.

Badami Caves

Ancient rock-cut temples famous for their carvings and sculptures dating back to the 6th century.
