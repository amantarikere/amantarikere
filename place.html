Explore Karnataka Tourism

Karnataka, the southern jewel of India, offers a rich blend of heritage, culture, and natural wonders. From ancient temples and historic ruins to scenic hill stations and pristine beaches, discover the best places to visit in Karnataka.

Mysore Palace

The Mysore Palace is an iconic symbol of Karnataka's royal heritage. Illuminated spectacularly at night, this Indo-Saracenic architectural marvel attracts millions of visitors annually.

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Hampi is renowned for its ancient ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire, offering spectacular stone temples, monuments, and breathtaking landscapes.

Coorg (Kodagu)

Known as the 'Scotland of India,' Coorg is famous for its lush coffee plantations, beautiful hills, and waterfalls, perfect for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

Badami Caves

Badami is home to ancient rock-cut cave temples dating back to the 6th century, showcasing exquisite carvings and a glimpse into Karnataka’s rich history.

Chikkamagaluru

Chikkamagaluru (also spelled Chikkamagalore) is a beautiful hill station town located in the state of Karnataka, India. It is famous for its coffee plantations, lush green mountains, cool climate, and trekking spots.

Shivamogga

Shivamogga (commonly known as Shimoga) is a scenic city in the Malnad region of Karnataka, India. Known as the "Gateway to the Western Ghats," it is famous for lush forests, waterfalls, wildlife, and historical significance.
